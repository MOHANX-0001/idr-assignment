About IDR

Where Academic
Insight Meets
Industry Practice.

The Institute of Digital Risk is an industry-led training and deployment institute built for the realities of digital, cyber and technology risk. We exist to close the capability gap between the talent market and the demands of regulated, high-consequence environments — from financial services and critical infrastructure to public sector and beyond.

Operating at the intersection of a leading UK university partnership and live industry engagement, IDR gives practitioners, students and organisations a route into credible, applied digital risk expertise. Our programmes are shaped by the problems organisations actually face — not theoretical frameworks alone.

University Partnership

Our academic collaboration grounds every programme in research-backed methodology, ensuring practitioners develop deep analytical capability alongside vocational skills.

Industry-Led Design

Curriculum, projects and deployment pathways are co-designed with industry partners to reflect current threats, regulatory demands and emerging risks including AI governance.

Deployment Focus

IDR isn't just a training provider — we deploy trained practitioners directly into organisations, bridging recruitment and capability development in a single model.
